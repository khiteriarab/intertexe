How to use INTERTEXE

A practical guide to the INTERTEXE software — product intelligence infrastructure for fashion. One governed product record powers passports, compliance readiness, consumer experiences, and resale.

What INTERTEXE is

INTERTEXE is not merely a Digital Product Passport database. It turns fragmented product, material, supplier and manufacturing data into one governed record — then publishes that record as the digital product experience your customer actually sees.

Govern Connect PLM, ERP, spreadsheets and supplier files into one structured product record with evidence, provenance and approval workflow.
Publish Turn approved data into passports, regulatory readiness and consumer-ready product experiences.
Deliver Hosted passport, white-label domain, or headless API into your existing app — from the same record.

How teams use the software

1. Bring the catalog in

Start with the files you already have. Upload or sync product rows, compositions, origins and supplier notes. INTERTEXE preserves original source values while normalizing materials for downstream use.

2. Resolve gaps and conflicts

Missing composition totals, conflicting fiber strings and incomplete origin fields become an issues inbox — not another spreadsheet. Approve an evidence-backed canonical record your teams can trust.

3. Benchmark materials

Compare fiber mix, completeness and passport readiness against governed peer segments. Use conversion signals to see which compositions and categories outperform.

4. Publish the passport

Choose your carrier and delivery mode. Preview the consumer experience, then publish Digital Product Passports from the same approved record — hosted by INTERTEXE, on your domain, or via API.

5. Extend into next life

When a garment moves on, the passport stays useful: resale value signals, route comparison, ownership transfer and circular options attached to the same product identity.

Delivery modes

  • Hosted — INTERTEXE templates; QR resolves to a passport we host.
  • White label — Same data on passport.yourbrand.com.
  • Headless API — Pull approved passport data into your app and tools.

Lifecycle at a glance

Manufacture → Trace → Passport → Environmental impact → Purchase → Own / repair → Resale value → Resale → Next owner → End of life.

QR / NFC / RFID identifies the product and resolves to the live passport — certifications, calculations, lifecycle events and resale history can evolve after manufacture.
