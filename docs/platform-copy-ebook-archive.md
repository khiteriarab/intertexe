## Positioning — what INTERTEXE is

INTERTEXE is **product intelligence infrastructure for fashion** — not merely a DPP compliance database.

We don't just help you organize the information a Digital Product Passport requires. We turn that information into the **digital product experience your customer actually sees** — hosted by INTERTEXE, on your domain, or inside your existing app.

### Three value pillars

**Govern** — Connect PLM, ERP, spreadsheets and supplier files into one structured product record — with evidence, provenance, and approval workflow.

**Publish** — Turn approved data into passports, regulatory readiness, and consumer-ready product experiences — not just compliance fields in a dashboard.

**Deliver** — Hosted passport, white-label domain, or headless API into your existing app. One governed record powers every channel.

## Four SaaS engines (full-circle architecture)

1. **Product Intelligence** — materials, composition, manufacturing, certifications  
2. **Compliance & Traceability** — DPP, supply chain, regulatory data, provenance  
3. **Environmental Intelligence** — French environmental cost (Ecobalyse), carbon/water metrics, traceability scoring — not a vague green score  
4. **Circularity & Residual Value** — repair, ownership, resale value, resale orchestration, ownership transfer  

**Lifecycle:** Manufacture → Trace → Passport → Environmental impact → Purchase → Own/repair → Resale value → Resale → Next owner → End of life  

**Identity rule:** QR / NFC / RFID identifies the product and resolves to the **live passport** — certifications, calculations, lifecycle events, and resale history can evolve after manufacture.

## Delivery modes

You don't have to send customers to an INTERTEXE-looking page.

Already have the app and website? INTERTEXE powers the product experience inside your existing digital ecosystem. Smaller brands can choose hosted passports with zero development. Enterprise brands can run all three delivery modes from the same governed record — QR on the garment, API in the app, white-label on the web.

### Hosted (Platform)
INTERTEXE templates — Editorial, Trace, Essential, Circular. QR resolves to a beautiful passport we host — zero development. Example: `intertexe.com/p/ABC123`

### White label (Professional · Enterprise)
Same templates and governed data on `passport.yourbrand.com`. Your customer never needs to know INTERTEXE hosts the infrastructure.

### Headless API (Enterprise)
Pull approved passport data via API into your existing app, website, and service tools. INTERTEXE owns the data cloud — you own the presentation. Example: `GET /v1/products/ABC123/passport`

## Resale / circularity (not a marketplace)

INTERTEXE is lifecycle infrastructure: manufacture → ownership → valuation → next owner.

- **Resale Value** — estimated current value, value retention, market demand, typical selling time  
- **Best Resale Route** — marketplace listing vs instant buyout vs consignment vs brand trade-in  
- Brand never receives consumer marketplace credentials  
- Sale writes back: ownership transfer, value retention, lifecycle event

## Environmental / sustainability (three scores)

Never one vague green score.

1. **Regulatory environmental score** — e.g. France Ecobalyse impact points with full methodology provenance  
2. **INTERTEXE traceability score** — how much of the supply chain can be **proven**  
3. **Consumer sustainability profile** — separate dimensions: traceability, materials, carbon, water, durability, repairability, circularity  

**Brand sustainability performance:** % fully traceable · avg traceability · natural materials · supply-chain disclosure · avg environmental cost · avg carbon — aggregated from product evidence. *Prove it, don't claim it.*
